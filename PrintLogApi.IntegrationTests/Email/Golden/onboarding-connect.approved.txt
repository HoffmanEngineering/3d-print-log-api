Typing in every print gets old fast. Connect the tool you already use and new prints show up on their own:

- OctoPrint: install the webhook plugin. https://www.3dprintlog.test/docs/octoprint-webhook
- Klipper (Moonraker): add one block to your config. https://www.3dprintlog.test/docs/klipper
- Your slicer: upload straight from PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer or SuperSlicer. https://www.3dprintlog.test/docs/slic3r-uploader

Each one takes about five minutes.
